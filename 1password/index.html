4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Price-hike backlash is the dominant 2026 sentiment theme

The March 27, 2026 increase (Individual to ~$47.88/yr, +33%; Families +20%) drives the dominant Reddit thread pattern — "should I cancel after the hike" — with a visible refugee flow to Apple Passwords, Bitwarden and Proton Pass. Engines citing reddit.com (19×) will absorb this framing. → Fix: publish an owned, dated "what you get at the new price" value page and a migration-cost comparison; brief support/community teams to answer hike threads with linkable facts.

Pricing-spread risk: third parties answer the pricing question, with conflicting numbers

In the scan, OpenAI answered "How much does 1Password Business cost?" citing comparedge.com and makerdeck.co — not 1password.com. Search shows a wall of third-party pricing pages (costbench, checkthat.ai, vendr, scribehow) quoting $7.99/user and a $19.95 Teams pack with varying details and negotiation claims. → Fix: make /pricing the most extractable pricing source on the web — plain-text per-plan table, dated "last updated," FAQ markup — so engines stop outsourcing the answer.

Competitor-owned comparison content holds ~67 citations

securden.com (28), bitwarden.com (27) and lastpass.com/blog (12) together out-cite every neutral reviewer except YouTube. When a buyer asks "1Password vs X," engines are frequently reading the competitor's version of the matchup. → Fix: expand 1password.com/compare into per-competitor, answer-first pages (vs Bitwarden, LastPass, Keeper, Dashlane, and a PAM-vs-EPM explainer aimed at the Securden/CyberArk framing).

Breach-question framing is good but fragile

Engines currently answer "Has 1Password ever been breached?" correctly (2023 Okta incident, no user data accessed) — but they assemble it from Ars Technica and Forbes, not from an owned canonical page. One stale or sloppy third-party rewrite could flip this answer. → Fix: maintain a dated, crawlable "Security incidents and our track record" page on 1password.com that states the Okta facts and the zero-vault-breach record explicitly.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

1Password is the AI-answer incumbent in this category — 88.4% presence, the largest owned-citation share, and a near-perfect crawl posture. Where it loses is the money question: pricing and comparison answers are being written by affiliates and competitors at the exact moment a +33% price hike dominates UGC sentiment. The single highest-leverage move: own the pricing and "vs" answers — an extractable, dated pricing page plus per-competitor comparison pages — before the hike narrative hardens into the engines' default framing.

4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Discover-stage invisibility: 18.9% — the rebrand outran the engines

Aviatrix shows in only 7 of 37 discover-stage answers. The company has repositioned from "multicloud networking" to "cloud network security / CNSF" (a category it invented in July 2025), but engines still frame category questions with AWS Transit Gateway, Cisco and Azure vWAN — and CNSF is not yet a term buyers ask about. → Fix: bridge content that ranks for the words buyers actually use ("multicloud networking," "cloud network security," "Transit Gateway alternative") and maps them to CNSF — don't wait for the market to learn the new vocabulary.

GPTBot is blocked while every other AI crawler is welcomed

robots.txt allows ClaudeBot, Perplexity, Google-Extended and CCBot but sets GPTBot: Disallow: /. ChatGPT can browse on request (OAI-SearchBot allowed) but the site is excluded from OpenAI's index/training corpus — a strange carve-out for a company that ships llms.txt. OpenAI was Aviatrix's best engine in the scan (66.7%) despite this. → Fix: confirm this is a deliberate policy; if not, allow GPTBot — one robots.txt line.

Pricing answers are assembled from marketplace meter rates and a 2021 PDF

With no public pricing page, Perplexity answers "How much does Aviatrix cost?" with AWS Marketplace per-hour line items ($0.23/tunnel-hr, $0.045/VPN-user-hr…), and search still surfaces a 2021 Aviatrix pricing sheet PDF hosted by a partner (criticalstart.com). Reviews add "cost is on the higher side." Buyers get stale, fragmented, sticker-shock numbers. → Fix: publish an owned pricing-model explainer (how billing units work, typical deployment ranges, TCO framing) and request takedown/update of the stale partner PDF.

Legacy footprint and animated stats leak stale or empty facts

legacy.docs.aviatrix.com is still cited 7× (deprecated architecture answers), and key homepage proof stats render as "0" in crawled HTML because they're JS-animated counters — a crawler literally reads "0 Credential types targeted" and "0% average savings." → Fix: server-render the real numbers (progressive enhancement for animation) and 301/banner legacy docs to current equivalents.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

Aviatrix wins decisively once a buyer knows its name — 100% presence on validate prompts, strong docs, the best llms.txt in this portfolio, and a 4.8 Gartner rating. It loses the top of the funnel: 18.9% discover-stage presence, because engines still describe the category in pre-rebrand vocabulary and competitor/native-cloud terms. The single highest-leverage move: publish bridge content that owns the old-language category queries ("multicloud networking," "Transit Gateway alternative," "cloud network security vendors") and routes them to the CNSF story — plus unblock GPTBot, which costs one line.

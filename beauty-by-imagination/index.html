4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

1. Pricing hallucination: engines quote "$84.60–$108.00" for BBI products

In the scan, Perplexity answered the price-range prompt with "$84.60 to $108.00" pulled from a Walmart marketplace result — roughly 5–10x the real price of a Wet Brush ($10–20) or Goody accessory ($5–15). With no pricing anchor anywhere on owned domains, engines grab whatever listing surfaces. → Fix: publish a "Our brands & typical price ranges" block on bbicompany.com and per-brand pricing pages on wetbrush.com/goody DTC sites; audit the Walmart seller page.

2. Zero visibility at the discover stage (0/24 answers)

BBI never appears in unbranded discovery prompts ("best detangling brush," "best hair accessories"); Revlon (8) and Conair (7) own those answers. The corporate site has no category/educational content for engines to cite. → Fix: publish definitional, answer-first category content on the brand sites (Wet Brush owns "detangling," Ouidad owns "curly hair method") and cross-link to the BBI entity.

3. Durability-complaint theme in UGC: bristle tips and paint failures

Search of review/forum content surfaces a recurring theme: Wet Brush bristle tips breaking off after a few months, paint peeling on minis, and Goody pins falling out — alongside strong positive detangler reviews. Engines already cite mixed-quality framing in "are they good quality" answers. → Fix: publish a crawlable warranty/quality page and FAQ ("how long does a Wet Brush last," replacement policy) so brand-owned text answers the durability question first.

4. Entity fragmentation: parent vs brands vs data-brokers

Engines stitch BBI's identity from zoominfo/contactout/legal deal pages; one scan answer even hedged on whether Curls and ACE are still in the portfolio. No llms.txt, no verified meta description, and the parent/brand relationship is only stated in one homepage paragraph. → Fix: ship llms.txt with the canonical brand list, founding facts, and retail channels; add Organization schema linking parent ↔ brand domains (pending script-layer check).

6Strategic insight — the pitch

BBI wins decisively on branded validation prompts (100%) because its own homepage paragraph is the answer engines quote — but it is invisible in discovery (0/24), where the category is decided and Revlon/Conair get named instead. The single highest-leverage move: build answer-first category content on the consumer brand sites (Wet Brush = detangling, Ouidad = curls) with explicit "a Beauty by Imagination brand" entity links, so discovery-stage citations flow back to the portfolio. Fixing the marketplace-driven pricing hallucination is the fastest trust win.

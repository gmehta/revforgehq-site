4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Engines give contradictory eligibility facts: $10K/mo vs $100K/mo

In the scan, OpenAI answered "revenue threshold" with $10,000/month citing a help.clear.co article, while Perplexity answered $100,000/month for 12+ months citing newer pages — a 10× discrepancy on the most basic qualification fact. The crawled homepage confirms $100K/mo is current; stale owned help docs are feeding the wrong answer.
→ Fix: audit and update/redirect every help.clear.co eligibility article to the current criteria; engines treat the help center as ground truth.

Fee spread and effective-APR criticism define the cost narrative

Third parties quote Clearco fees anywhere from 3.5% to 16% flat, and some compute "effective APR above 45%" for fast repayers; one review also claims a "$10,000 deposit fee" and borrower-paid legal fees. With no published owned rate grid, these hostile/affiliate numbers are what AI answers serve.
→ Fix: publish the "New Pricing Grid" referenced on the homepage as a crawlable pricing page with worked examples (fee, term, weekly payment), and link it from llms.txt.

Repayment-pressure and support complaints persist in cited UGC

Search and scan verbatims surface "aggressive daily repayment schedules… crushing cash flow during slow periods," "long wait times to resolve issues," stricter underwriting after 2023–24 restructuring, and Trustpilot at 3.9/5. Engines quote these themes directly in "is Clearco legit/worth it" answers.
→ Fix: publish a plain-language repayment mechanics page (capped weekly remittances, early-payment option) and run a Trustpilot response/refresh program.

Entity confusion: Clearbanc legacy + "ClearCompany" collisions

The Trustpilot profile lives at clearbanc.com, login is my.clearbanc.com, and searches for "ClearCo pricing 2026" return ClearCompany (HR software) pages on Capterra/GetApp — partners.clearcompany.com even appears in Clearco's own citation set. Engines are partially blending three entities.
→ Fix: consistent Organization schema (Clear Finance Technology Corporation, formerly Clearbanc, brand "Clearco") and reclaim/rename legacy review profiles.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

Clearco's technical AEO foundation is among the strongest we've scanned — open robots, deep llms.txt, dated content, answer-first homepage — and it wins validate prompts (100%). It loses at discovery (29.2%), where competitor listicles (Onramp, Luca, Founderpath, Capchase) own "best ecommerce funding" answers, and on cost, where unpublished pricing lets critics set the effective-APR narrative. Highest-leverage move: ship an owned, crawlable pricing/terms page and fix the $10K-vs-$100K eligibility contradiction in the help center — Clearco's biggest AI-answer errors are coming from its own stale pages.

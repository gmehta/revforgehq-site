4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Entity collision: "Miva" = Miva Open University (Nigeria) in Perplexity

On the pricing prompt, Perplexity answered with Miva Open University tuition (₦340,000/yr) before the ecommerce platform — a direct entity-disambiguation failure on a core buying question. → Fix: add Organization JSON-LD with sameAs links (Wikipedia/Wikidata/LinkedIn/Crunchbase) and reinforce "Miva, Inc — ecommerce platform, San Diego" entity signals across owned pages and llms.txt.

Pricing-spread risk: $29/user vs $79.99/mo vs $1,500/mo in AI answers

Because Miva publishes no prices, engines stitch together conflicting third-party figures: aggregators list ~$29/user/mo, others $79.99/mo entry and ~$1,500/mo at $1M revenue, plus hourly consulting rates. Buyers get a 50x spread. → Fix: publish an authoritative revenue-based pricing explainer (bands + what's included) on /miva-ecommerce-pricing so engines quote Miva, not aggregators.

Complaint themes engines already echo: rising costs, dated admin, expensive integrations

Review-site research (Capterra, TrustRadius, G2, SoftwareAdvice) surfaces consistent criticism: pricing creep vs competitors, an admin UI that "feels older," steep learning curve, and costly third-party integrations vs Shopify/Magento app stores. Perplexity already qualifies "not always the easiest choice." → Fix: publish a modern-admin / Miva 10 UX page and an integrations-cost transparency page; respond to negative TrustRadius reviews.

Stale-fact citation: 2014-era PA-DSS press release answering 2026 PCI questions

OpenAI's PCI answer cites a press release about Miva Merchant 9.0 PA-DSS validation — a decade-old artifact standing in for current PCI DSS 4.0 posture. → Fix: publish a dated, current security/compliance page (PCI DSS 4.0, SAQ-A guidance) and let the old press page 301 or reference it.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

Miva wins decisively at the validate stage (100% presence — its own FAQ page, KB and blog are the citations) and holds its own in comparisons, but is invisible in discovery: 0% presence across 24 unbranded "best ecommerce platform" prompts, where BigCommerce, Shopify Plus and Magento dominate. The site is technically AI-ready (open robots, llms.txt, crawlable claims), so the single highest-leverage move is content, not plumbing: publish owned, citable pricing and "best platform for high-SKU / B2B / auto-parts" listicle-style content that gives engines a Miva source to cite in discovery answers.

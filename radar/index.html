Questions

The fine print, answered

What exactly is AEO / GEO?+
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) are the practice of getting your brand accurately recommended inside AI answers — the way SEO got you into blue links. As buyers shift from Googling to asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, being cited (and cited correctly) is the new front page. Radar measures and improves it.
How is the free trial different from the free plan?+
When you sign up you get 7 days of full Pro — daily scans, all four engines, 50 prompts, competitors, and hallucination detection. When the trial ends, you can subscribe to Pro, or drop to the Free plan (a weekly single-engine scan of 15 prompts) and keep watching your brand at no cost, forever. No card is required at any point unless you choose to upgrade.
Which engines do you actually query?+
ChatGPT (with web search), Perplexity, Google Gemini (with grounding), and Google AI Overviews. These four bundle the leading models and the leading retrieval systems, which is where your buyers' answers really come from. We pin model versions where the engines allow it, so a silent model upgrade doesn't corrupt your trend line.
How do you catch hallucinations?+
You keep a short, versioned facts store — real prices, plan names, key claims. Every answer the engines give is automatically checked against it. Conflicts are confidence-scored and flagged with the exact engine, prompt, and timestamp. Low-confidence calls go to human review before they're ever shown as a "gotcha," so you only see real errors.
Why daily? Isn't that overkill?+
40–60% of the sources engines cite change every month, and model answers drift day to day. A weekly snapshot misses the swing that lost you the week. Radar runs daily on Pro so you catch a new competitor comparison page or a fresh hallucination the morning it happens — not at your next monthly review.
How is this different from Profound, Otterly, or other trackers?+
The trackers tell you whether you were mentioned. Radar carries through to accuracy (hallucination detection vs. your real facts), rigor (multi-sample statistical confidence, not single-shot), and action (a 45-point on-site audit, competitor teardowns, and a ranked fix queue). It's built by an AI-RevOps consultancy that does this for Fortune-500 brands by hand — Radar is that work, automated.
Is my data isolated and secure?+
Yes. Every tenant's data is logically isolated at the database layer, encrypted at rest and in transit, and your reports sit behind authentication and expiring links — never a guessable public URL. The only data shared across customers is public-web competitor crawl results, which keeps everyone's scans fast and cheap without exposing anything private.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Anytime, in one click. The trial never auto-charges without your action, and we'll remind you two days before it ends. Cancel and you keep access through the period you've paid for, then drop to the Free plan automatically.

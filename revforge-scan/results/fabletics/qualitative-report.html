Brand Sentinel — risk findings

❌ Stale membership pricing across citable sources

Current 2026 listicles cite "$60/month membership"; older widely-indexed sources say $49.95. The live site charges $69.95 on the 6th of each month (verified on homepage today). An engine answering "how much is Fabletics VIP membership" will likely quote a wrong, lower number — manufacturing the exact "surprise charge" complaint that drives the scam narrative.

→ Fix: canonical "How membership works" page with current pricing in text + FAQPage/Offer schema; correction outreach to top-cited listicles; llms.txt facts block.

❌ The billing-trap narrative owns validation prompts

"Is Fabletics legit / a scam" answers will draw from Reddit, Trustpilot, BBB and 2015-era press about the CA district-attorney investigation and hard cancellations. Fabletics has since added checkout warnings and online cancellation — but no authoritative, crawlable page tells that story, so engines cite the old one.

→ Fix: answer-first transparency hub ("Is Fabletics a subscription? How do I cancel?" in plain text), published cancellation stats, FTC click-to-cancel compliance statement.

❌ A top-5 DTC activewear site with zero structured data

No JSON-LD, no og tags, no H1 on the homepage (DOM-verified). Product, Offer, AggregateRating and FAQ schema are absent exactly where engines look for machine-readable truth — so they reconstruct Fabletics from third parties instead.

→ Fix: schema rollout across homepage + PDPs + membership page; H1/heading hierarchy; alt text (88 images missing).

⚠️ Product strength is real but entangled with membership caveats

Fabletics IS featured in 2026 "best leggings" lists (PowerHold gets genuine praise) — discover-stage presence is likely decent. But nearly every favorable mention carries a "watch the subscription" caveat, which AI answers amplify by merging product praise with billing warnings into a single recommendation.

→ Fix: decouple narratives — strengthen product-only content (fabric tech, fit guides) while fixing the membership answer separately.

Strategic insight

Fabletics' AI problem is the inverse of most brands': access is wide open and product sentiment is genuinely favorable, but the membership-billing answer is authored entirely by critics — and the company's own site gives engines almost nothing (no schema, no H1, promo-wall copy) to counter it. The single highest-leverage move: publish the definitive, crawlable, schema-marked "How Fabletics membership works" answer — current $69.95 price, skip window, online cancellation — then push that truth into the listicles engines already cite. Fix the membership answer and the favorable product coverage starts working unencumbered.

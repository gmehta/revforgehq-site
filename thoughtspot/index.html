4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Pricing-spread risk: $25/user list vs ~$137K average contract — engines quote both, side by side

Perplexity's answer to "How much does ThoughtSpot cost per user?" cites the $25/$50 list price, then immediately caveats with Vendr's "$92,522 median / $137,000 average" contract data. Competitor blogs (Toucan Toco, Luzmo, Mammoth, Embeddable) push narratives of "$100K–$1M annually," "$5–6 per dashboard load," and "$355K–$650K first-year cost." Buyers asking AI get a 5,000x price spread.
→ Fix: publish a crawlable pricing-explainer FAQ ("What does a typical mid-market ThoughtSpot contract cost?") with honest ranges, so the owned page — not competitor estimates — anchors the number.

Discover-stage invisibility: 29.2% presence vs 94–100% in compare/validate

The scan shows ThoughtSpot dominates once it is named in the prompt, but appears in fewer than a third of category-level "best BI / analytics tool" discovery answers, where Power BI (28 mentions), Tableau (27) and Looker (23) crowd it out.
→ Fix: target definitional discovery content ("agentic analytics," "AI analyst agent," "search-driven BI") and third-party listicle placement — the channel engines actually cite at discover stage.

Complaint themes engines can repeat: data-prep burden, slow support, embed customization limits

Search surfaces recurring criticism: search quality depends on heavy worksheet modeling first ("needs someone technical to structure it"), slow support response, and embedded dashboards that are hard to match to product design language — much of it hosted on competitor blogs that engines cite.
→ Fix: publish SpotterModel-era rebuttal content (automated modeling directly answers the data-prep complaint) and quantified support SLAs in crawlable text.

Pricing citations are competitor-owned

Of the sources engines used for ThoughtSpot pricing answers, the majority are rival vendors (Luzmo, Toucan Toco, Mammoth, Embeddable, ITQlick) — competitors literally write the brand's cost narrative.
→ Fix: expand thoughtspot.com/pricing with FAQ-formatted, extraction-friendly answers ("Is ThoughtSpot worth it for a mid-size company?") mirroring the exact validate prompts in the scan.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

ThoughtSpot is one of the most AEO-ready sites we have audited — a model llms.txt, open AI-crawler policy, fresh metadata — and it shows: 100% presence on validate prompts. The exposure is upstream and off-site: at discover stage it appears in only 29% of answers while Power BI and Tableau dominate, and its pricing/criticism narrative is authored largely by competitor blogs that engines cite. The single highest-leverage move is owning the pricing-and-worth narrative with extraction-ready FAQ content on owned pages, paired with discovery-stage placement in the third-party roundups engines lean on.

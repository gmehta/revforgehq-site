4Brand Sentinel — what AI likely tells your buyers Risk findings

Acquisition-status confusion: the site says Google, AI answers may not

Wiz.io's own footer now routes its Privacy Policy to Google Cloud's acquisitions privacy notice — on-site evidence the Google deal has closed — yet nothing in homepage body copy states the Google Cloud relationship, and engine answers in our scan describe Wiz as a standalone vendor. Buyers asking "is Wiz part of Google?" or "will Wiz stay multi-cloud after Google?" get inconsistent answers, and competitors (Orca, Prisma, CrowdStrike) are actively seeding "Google lock-in" doubt.
→ Fix: publish a definitive, crawlable "Wiz + Google Cloud: what changes and what doesn't" FAQ (multi-cloud commitment, AWS/Azure support, roadmap) and reference it from llms.txt.

Pricing-opacity risk: custom-quote page leaves a $24K–$354K narrative vacuum

Wiz's /pricing page is quote-only, so engines answer cost questions from third parties: AWS Marketplace examples ($24K Essential / $38K Advanced per 100 workloads), Vendr ($111,500 median, range $24,005–$354,350), and even a third-party domain literally named wizpricing.com. The brand's price story is written entirely off-site.
→ Fix: add a crawlable pricing-model explainer (workload-based units, module bundles, example tiers) so wiz.io anchors the numbers engines repeat.

Proof metrics invisible to crawlers — homepage outcomes render as "0%"

The "Driving Outcomes" section's headline stats are JS-animated counters that appear as 0% in static HTML — AI crawlers literally read "0% of customers achieve 0 criticals." The strongest quantified claims on the page (MTTR reduction, dev adoption) cannot be lifted into answers.
→ Fix: render the real numbers in static text (or noscript/JSON-LD) so claims are extractable.

Competitor blogs dominate the non-owned citation footprint

Of the top 25 cited domains, at least 14 are direct competitors or challenger vendors (Orca, Palo Alto, SentinelOne, Sysdig, Cycode, Aqua, CrowdStrike, Aikido, AccuKnox, Check Point, Trend Micro, Fortinet, Fidelis, Tamnoon) — far outnumbering neutral review/authority sources. At discover stage Wiz appears in only 54.2% of answers, so the framing in those answers is heavily competitor-authored.
→ Fix: invest in neutral-territory presence (TechTarget-class media, G2/Gartner review velocity, analyst briefings) and Wiz-owned "alternatives/comparison" pages targeting the same queries.

6Strategic insight — the pitch

Wiz is technically near-perfect for AEO — open crawler policy, answer-first copy, fresh dated content, an Academy engines already cite — and it shows: 100% presence on compare and validate prompts. Its exposure is narrative, not technical: a quote-only pricing page and silence about the Google acquisition leave the two questions buyers most ask AI ("what does Wiz cost?" and "what happens now that Google owns it?") to be answered by competitors and third parties. The single highest-leverage move is publishing owned, crawlable answers to the Google-acquisition and pricing-model questions — plus an llms.txt — before competitor framing hardens into the models.
